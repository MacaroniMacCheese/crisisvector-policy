Crisis Vector — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

WHO WE ARE

Crisis Vector is a strategy game made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. For questions, email crisisvectors@gmail.com (also shown on the game's Google Play page).

WHAT WE COLLECT

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information ourselves, and the game has no servers of its own. It has no chat and no purchases. Your campaign progress and settings are saved on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also kept in your Google account (see GOOGLE PLAY GAMES). The save file on your device is sealed with a key held in your phone's secure hardware so that it cannot be changed outside the game; that key never leaves your device.

ADS (UNITY ADS)

Crisis Vector is free and shows ads from Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies: a banner at the bottom of the screen on menus and in battles, a full-screen ad after each campaign mission, and optional rewarded videos that only play when you tap WATCH. The ad service only starts after you have answered the ad question. While it is active, Unity Ads may collect: device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android advertising ID), approximate location (worked out from your IP address), basic device information (model, operating system, language, network type), how you interact with an ad while it plays (views and taps), app diagnostics and in-app purchase history (Crisis Vector has no purchases). Unity uses this to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, only if you allow it, to show personalised ads. The data is sent encrypted. Unity's own privacy policy explains how it handles this data and how to ask Unity to delete it: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

GOOGLE PLAY GAMES

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the game signs you in automatically; you can also sign in from Settings. While you are signed in, your Play Games player ID and gamer name are shared with the game, and a copy of your progress (DD, research, missions, medals, veterans and records) is stored in Google Play Games Saved Games so you can restore it on another device. Google stores this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We never receive it on a server of our own. You can play without signing in, and you can delete the game's Play Games data or your whole Play Games profile at https://play.google.com/games/profile or in your Google Account.

YOUR CHOICES

In Settings you can switch PERSONALISED ADS on or off at any time, and you can hide the reward ad icons (REWARD AD ICONS off); the banner and the end-of-mission ad still show. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

UPDATE CHECK

When you open the game, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. Google Play handles that check under its own terms and uses your device details and app version to answer it. We never receive this information.

INTERNET AND PERMISSIONS

The game plays offline. It only uses the internet to fetch ads, to back up your progress to Google Play Games while you are signed in, and to check Google Play for a newer version. It never asks for your contacts, camera, microphone, files or precise location.

CHILDREN

Crisis Vector is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version is published in the game and at the same web address. Last updated: 7 November 2026.
