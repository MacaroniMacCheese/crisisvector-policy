Crisis Vector — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

WHO WE ARE

Crisis Vector is a strategy game made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. For questions, email shellawilliam196@gmail.com (also shown on the game's Google Play page).

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no chat and no purchases. Your campaign progress and settings are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

OPTIONAL ADS (UNITY ADS)

The game can show optional rewarded video ads from Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies. An ad only plays when you tap WATCH, and the ad service only starts after you have answered the ad question. While it is active, Unity Ads may collect: device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android advertising ID), approximate location (worked out from your IP address), basic device information (model, operating system, language, network type), how you interact with an ad while it plays (views and taps), app diagnostics and in-app purchase history (Crisis Vector has no purchases). Unity uses this to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, only if you allow it, to show personalised ads. The data is sent encrypted. Unity's own privacy policy explains how it handles this data and how to ask Unity to delete it: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

YOUR CHOICES

In Settings you can switch PERSONALISED ADS on or off at any time, and you can hide every ad icon (REWARD AD ICONS off). With the icons off, no ad is ever shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

INTERNET AND PERMISSIONS

The game plays fully offline. It only uses the internet to fetch optional ads. It never asks for your contacts, camera, microphone, files or precise location.

CHILDREN

Crisis Vector is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version is published in the game and at the same web address. Last updated: 15 October 2026.
